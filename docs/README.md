# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation describes OctoAcme’s lightweight, customer-first approach to delivering projects through clear ownership, iterative work, and data-informed decisions. Work moves from **initiation**, where teams agree on the problem, outcomes, stakeholders, and initial risks, into **planning**, where they define scope, an actionable backlog, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and milestones.

During **execution and tracking**, teams use a project board, regular delivery rhythms, small reviewed changes, and testing to make progress visible and maintain quality. Risks and dependencies are monitored throughout the lifecycle, with regular stakeholder updates and clear escalation paths. Before **release and deployment**, teams confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, and rollback plans; after deployment, they verify and communicate the result. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents turn lessons into owned improvement actions.

The process relies on clear roles: the Project Manager (PM) coordinates delivery, risks, and communication; the Product Manager (PdM) or Product Lead defines outcomes and priorities; developers and QA/testing build and validate the work; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
