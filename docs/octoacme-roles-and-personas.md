# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Manager / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate day-to-day delivery across teams, while Release Managers coordinate release readiness and deployment. One person may cover both responsibilities.

### Responsibilities
- Track delivery progress, dependencies, and impediments with the Project Manager
- Coordinate release scope, timing, readiness checks, and deployment communications
- Confirm that testing, release notes, support plans, and rollback plans are ready
- Coordinate post-release verification and follow-up actions

### Interactions
- Partner with the Project Manager on milestones, risks, and delivery status
- Align release scope and priorities with the Product Manager
- Coordinate readiness and deployment tasks with Developers and QA/Testing
- Keep Stakeholders informed about release timing, outcomes, and issues

### Lifecycle Fit
Work throughout execution and tracking, with focused coordination during release preparation, deployment, and post-release follow-up.

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs and business goals into clear requirements the delivery team can understand and validate.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, and constraints
- Refine requirements and acceptance criteria with the Product Manager
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and dependencies in proposed solutions
- Support validation that delivered work meets agreed business needs

### Interactions
- Work with Stakeholders to understand needs and confirm requirements
- Partner with the Product Manager to refine scope and acceptance criteria
- Clarify requirements for Developers and QA/Testing throughout delivery
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to surface dependencies and decisions

### Lifecycle Fit
Contribute during initiation and planning to define needs, then support execution, testing, and acceptance.

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads guide user experience and interaction design so that solutions are useful, accessible, and consistent with user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and map key journeys
- Create and validate designs, prototypes, and interaction patterns
- Incorporate usability and accessibility considerations into design decisions
- Support design handoff and review implemented experiences

### Interactions
- Collaborate with the Product Manager on user needs, priorities, and outcomes
- Work with Stakeholders and users to validate needs and design concepts
- Partner with Developers to communicate designs and resolve implementation questions
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility checks

### Lifecycle Fit
Contribute from discovery and planning through implementation, validation, and post-release feedback.

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Reviewers identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements and help teams address risks and controls.

### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant security, privacy, and compliance requirements early
- Review designs and changes for risks, control gaps, and required evidence
- Recommend mitigations and track required approvals or follow-up actions
- Confirm required checks are complete before release

### Interactions
- Advise the Project Manager on security or compliance risks, dependencies, and approvals
- Work with the Product Manager and Stakeholders to clarify obligations and risk acceptance
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to validate controls and checks
- Coordinate with the Delivery / Release Manager on release gates and readiness

### Lifecycle Fit
Engage during initiation and planning to identify requirements, review changes throughout delivery, and confirm applicable checks before release.
